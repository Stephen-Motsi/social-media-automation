SOCIAL MEDIA AUTOMATION - SYSTEM OVERVIEW

Purpose
This web app lets staff create and manage adverts, schedule social posts, and
deliver relevant offers to subscribers.

Roles
- Users sign in and subscribe to listed adverts on selected platforms. They can
  manage their profile, WhatsApp link, subscriptions, and sessions.
- Admins manage adverts, social channels, subscribers, approvals, and publishing.
- Super admins have the admin capabilities plus access to user management,
  audit logs, role management, and the super-admin dashboard.

Main features
- Create adverts with captions and images, including AI-assisted content tools
  and checks against platform requirements.
- Connect Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, X, and TikTok accounts.
- Configure channels and recurring schedules.
- Publish posts and send WhatsApp messages through a background worker.
- Let account holders subscribe to adverts and let public subscribers receive
  relevant offers by WhatsApp.
- Review activity, sessions, notifications, and approval items according to
  the signed-in user's role.

Technology
- Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS for the web application.
- Auth.js for authentication and role-aware access checks.
- PostgreSQL and Prisma for application data.
- Redis and BullMQ for background and scheduled jobs.
- Platform APIs for publishing and messaging; OpenAI services for image
  generation and content moderation.

Key routes
- /                 Public advert listing and entry point.
- /login            Sign in and account creation.
- /me               User dashboard.
- /admin            Admin dashboard.
- /admin/super-admin Super-admin overview.
- /admin/users      Super-admin user management.
- /admin/logs       Super-admin activity and session logs.
- /admin/roles      Super-admin role management.
- /channels         Social account and schedule management.
- /audience         Subscriber and audience management.
- /approvals        Items requiring staff review.

The application checks access on the server, not only in the navigation.
Platform credentials and environment settings belong in local environment
configuration; do not store secrets in this overview file.
